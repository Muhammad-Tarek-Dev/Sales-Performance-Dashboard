# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive **Excel-based** reporting solution designed to analyze and monitor sales data efficiently. The dashboard delivers clear, visual insights into revenue, profit, costs, and overall sales trends to support data-driven decision-making.

![Sales Performance Dashboard](sales-dashboard.jpg)

---

## 🧩 Overview

The Sales Performance Dashboard consolidates key business metrics into a single, intuitive view. Built entirely in Microsoft Excel with Pivot Tables, Slicers, and Charts, it enables business stakeholders to quickly assess performance across regions, categories, channels, and salespeople — all without writing a single line of code.

---

## ✨ Features

- **KPI Summary Cards** — Instant visibility into Total Net Sales ($188,492), Total Profit ($40,147), and Total Orders (12)
- **Time Analysis Chart** — Line chart tracking monthly net sales trends across the full year
- **Sales Band Distribution** — Bar and line combo chart segmenting orders and revenue across Low, Medium, and High sales bands
- **Salesperson Performance** — Horizontal bar chart comparing each rep's net sales vs. profit contribution
- **Profit Analysis by Category** — Donut chart breaking down profit between Accessories and Electronics
- **Net Sales by Region & Channel** — Clustered bar chart comparing revenue across East, South, West, and North regions
- **Interactive Slicers** — Filter all charts simultaneously by Region, Category, Channel, and Salesperson

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites

- Microsoft Excel 2016 or later (recommended for full Slicer and PivotChart support)

### Usage

1. **Clone or download** this repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/Muhammad-Tarek-Dev/Sales-Performance-Dashboard.git
   ```
2. Open `Sales Performance Dashboard.xlsx` in Microsoft Excel.
3. Use the **slicers** on the left panel to filter the dashboard by:
   - Region (East, North, South)
   - Category (Accessories, Electronics)
   - Channel (Distributor, Online, Retail)
   - Salesperson (Ahmed, Lina, Mona, Omar, Sara)
4. All charts and KPI cards will update dynamically based on your selections.

## 📈 Dashboard Sections

| Section | Description |
|---|---|
| **Total Net Sales** | Overall revenue after discounts and returns |
| **Total Profit** | Net earnings after subtracting costs |
| **Total Orders** | Count of distinct orders in the selected view |
| **Time Analysis** | Monthly sales trend throughout the year |
| **Sales Band Distribution** | Order count and revenue split by deal size |
| **Salesperson Performance** | Individual rep comparison of sales and profit |
| **Profit by Category** | Category-level profit breakdown (Accessories vs. Electronics) |
| **Net Sales by Region & Channel** | Regional revenue across all sales channels |
